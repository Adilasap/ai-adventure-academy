AI Adventure Academy offers free live online AI workshops for school students. Workshop topics shown on this website are a proposed learning plan and may change. Schedules and other details are shared on WhatsApp.

Some activities may use third-party AI tools. Each tool has its own age requirements and terms of use, which students and parents should review. Tools will only be used where appropriate for the student's age.

Responsible use

AI-generated content can be incorrect. Students are encouraged to check important information and to use AI to support, not replace, their own thinking.
